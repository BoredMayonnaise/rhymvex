# Phase 4 — Scale

> **Goal:** Rhymvex operates as a structured agency — team in place, productized services, predictable revenue, and positioned as a recognized brand-system authority.

**Status:** ⏳ Planned
**Estimated Duration:** 3–6 months (ongoing)
**Preceded by:** [Phase 3 — Growth](./phase-3-growth.md)
**Followed by:** —

---

## Overview

Phase 4 is where Rhymvex becomes a real agency — not just a freelancer with a brand. Revenue is predictable, delivery doesn't depend solely on the founder, services are packaged into products, and the brand is a recognized name in its niche. Growth in this phase is about multiplying the system, not just adding more hours.

---

## Goals

- [ ] Revenue is predictable month-over-month (strong retainer base)
- [ ] Team hired or contractors systematized (delivery doesn't bottleneck on founder)
- [ ] Core services are fully productized with clear SOPs
- [ ] Rhymvex is a recognized name (media, community, referrals)
- [ ] Expanded offering: productized templates, digital products, or workshops
- [ ] New revenue stream(s) beyond project-based work

## Deliverables

By the end of Phase 4, the following must exist:

1. **Productized service menu** — 3–4 packages, self-serve intake, sales pages live
2. **Team / contractor network** — delivery not dependent solely on founder
3. **SOP library** — every core process documented and repeatable
4. **Digital product(s)** — at least 1 passive revenue stream live
5. **Authority signals** — press, mentions, podcast appearances, named framework
6. **Financial health** — predictable MRR, clear margins, 3-month runway
7. **Niche positioning** — clear ICP and vertical focus defined

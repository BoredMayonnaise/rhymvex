# Phase 2 — Launch

> **Goal:** Rhymvex is publicly live, posting consistently, attracting inbound interest, and closing its first 2–3 paying clients.

**Status:** ⏳ Planned
**Estimated Duration:** 4–6 weeks
**Preceded by:** [Phase 1 — Foundation](./phase-1-foundation.md)
**Followed by:** [Phase 3 — Growth](./phase-3-growth.md)

---

## Overview

Phase 2 is the ignition. With the foundation locked, this phase is about turning Rhymvex on publicly — consistent content, outbound outreach, and converting the first clients. The goal isn't perfection; it's proof. Every client in this phase becomes a case study. Every post builds the brand.

---

## Goals

- [ ] Consistent content cadence live across channels (min. 3–4 posts/week)
- [ ] First 2–3 paying clients signed and onboarded
- [ ] At least 1 completed case study published
- [ ] Email list / newsletter started
- [ ] First round of paid ads tested

## Deliverables

By the end of Phase 2, the following must exist:

1. **Content system running** — 2+ weeks planned, templates live, scheduling active
2. **First clients signed** — 2–3 paying clients onboarded
3. **First case study published** — real result, real metrics, on brand
4. **Email list started** — lead magnet live, welcome sequence running
5. **Ad test completed** — first paid test done, results logged
6. **Social following growing** — minimum baseline across channels established

- [ ] Content cadence: 3–4 posts/week for 4+ consecutive weeks

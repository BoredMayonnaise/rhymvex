# Phase 3 — Growth

> **Goal:** Rhymvex has a reliable, repeatable pipeline. Revenue is consistent. The brand is recognized in its niche. Case study library is building.

**Status:** ⏳ Planned
**Estimated Duration:** 2–3 months
**Preceded by:** [Phase 2 — Launch](./phase-2-launch.md)
**Followed by:** [Phase 4 — Scale](./phase-4-scale.md)

---

## Overview

Phase 3 is where Rhymvex transitions from "getting clients" to "attracting clients." The machine built in Phases 1–2 starts compounding — content drives inbound, case studies convert, and ads are dialled in. The focus shifts from survival to repeatability: a predictable pipeline, growing revenue, and a growing reputation.

---

## Goals

- [ ] Consistent monthly revenue (target: first recurring revenue milestone)
- [ ] 5+ published case studies across different client types
- [ ] Paid acquisition profitable (ROAS positive or CAC clearly understood)
- [ ] Content driving inbound enquiries (not just outbound)
- [ ] Retainer clients signed (recurring revenue begins)
- [ ] Referral system in place

## Deliverables

By the end of Phase 3, the following must exist:

1. **Case study library** — 5+ published studies with real metrics
2. **Profitable ad system** — winning creatives scaled, retargeting live
3. **Recurring revenue** — at least 1–2 retainer clients
4. **Sales playbook** — documented, repeatable, tracked in CRM
5. **Partnership network** — 1–2 active referral partners
6. **"Rhymvex Playbook"** — internal delivery framework documented
7. **Email list** — 200+ subscribers, monthly newsletter running

# Rhymvex — Client Intake Process

> **Purpose:** The repeatable system that turns a booked call into a started project. No client work begins until every step below is complete.
> **Owner:** Anyone at Rhymvex running a new engagement. If you're reading this, it's you.

---

## The Five Stages

| # | Stage | Exit condition |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Discovery call** | Call notes + brand audit summary written |
| 2 | **Scope & proposal** | Proposal sent, fixed price agreed |
| 3 | **Agreement** | 50% deposit received, contract signed |
| 4 | **Onboarding** | Asset access + kickoff deck complete |
| 5 | **Kickoff** | Kickoff call done, Phase 01 started |

---

## Stage 1 — Discovery Call (30 min)

**Goal:** Understand the problem well enough to price it — or say no.

**Before**
- [ ] Review the enquiry form responses (see below)
- [ ] Glance at their site + one social channel

**During — ask these, in order:**
1. "What does your brand need to do that it doesn't today?"
2. "What have you already tried?"
3. "What does success look like in 90 days?"
4. "What's the budget range for this?"
5. "Who else needs to say yes to this?"

**After (same day)**
- [ ] Write call notes into the project folder
- [ ] Send a 3-line summary email: what I heard, what I'd recommend, next step

**Red flags → walk away or renegotiate:**
- "We just need a logo" with no budget for a system
- No decision-maker on the call
- Scope already expanding before pricing

---

## Stage 2 — Scope & Proposal

**Goal:** A fixed price the client can approve in one read.

- [ ] Pick the package from `services-and-pricing.md` (or define a custom scope)
- [ ] Fill `proposal-template.md` — every bracket replaced
- [ ] Price check: deliverables ↔ price ↔ duration must be defensible
- [ ] Send as PDF within 48 hours of the call
- [ ] Follow up once after 5 business days if no reply

---

## Stage 3 — Agreement

**Goal:** Money and signature before any work starts. Non-negotiable.

- [ ] Contract sent (proposal + terms)
- [ ] 50% deposit invoiced and **received**
- [ ] Start date confirmed in writing
- [ ] Project folder created: `projects/[client]/` with notes, proposal, agreement

> No deposit, no work. Exceptions require two yeses — and there are no exceptions in Phase 1.

---

## Stage 4 — Onboarding

**Goal:** Everything we need, before we need it.

**Access checklist:**
- [ ] Brand assets (logos, fonts, guidelines) — current versions
- [ ] Platform access (social, analytics, CMS) — read-only is fine to start
- [ ] One brand contact with decision authority
- [ ] Slack/email channel agreed

**Kickoff deck prepared:**
- [ ] Restated problem (their words)
- [ ] Restated scope (our words)
- [ ] Timeline with dates
- [ ] Communication cadence (when we sync, how fast we reply)

---

## Stage 5 — Kickoff

**Goal:** Aligned expectations, then straight into Phase 01.

- [ ] Kickoff call: walk the deck, confirm the first deliverable date
- [ ] First deliverable date in both calendars
- [ ] Client knows exactly what to send us and by when

---

## Enquiry Form (send before every discovery call)

1. Company name and website
2. What does your brand need to do that it doesn't today?
3. What have you already tried?
4. Budget range (€1k–3k / €3k–8k / €8k+)
5. Ideal start date
6. Who else needs to say yes?

---

## Communication Rules

- **Reply within one business day** — even to say "I'll have that Thursday."
- **One idea per message.** Details go in the project folder, not the thread.
- **Scope changes go through a written change order** — new price, new date, then new work.
- **Approved CTAs only** in client-facing assets: "See our work" · "Book a call" · "Get your brand audit" · "Start your project" · "View case study" · "Download the framework".

> **Purpose:** First document a prospect sees after a discovery call. Fixed price, fixed scope, no surprises.
> **How to use:** Replace every `[BRACKETED]` value. Keep it under 2 pages. Send as PDF.
> **Brand rules:** Black/Slate background, White body text, Volt for the cover accent and CTA, Ember never. Inter for body, Space Grotesk for headings. Max 3 colours.

## Positioning

Most agencies hand you a logo. We hand you a playbook.

Rhymvex builds **brand systems** — the message, the rules, the tokens and the
templates that make every asset you ship afterwards consistent by default.
One system, repeated, instead of a folder of one-offs.

**We don't start with a package. We start with what's getting in your way.**

The three engagements below are the shapes we usually arrive at. They are the
output of a conversation, not the entrance to it. A visitor should be able to
arrive not knowing which one they need.

---

## How the Conversation Works

1. **The client's problem** — where the brand is stuck, in their words.
2. **Diagnosis** — what is actually causing it, not what the symptom looks like.
3. **Recommended engagement** — one of the three below, or none of them.
4. **Collaborative work** — built with the client, not presented to them.
5. **Implementation** — assets, system, or production, depending on scope.
6. **Review and improvement** — what is working, what is not, what changes.
7. **Ongoing support** — only if there is a reason for it.

> If we don't think you need one of these, we say so. That is the whole
> positioning: an advisor, not a storefront.

## Core Engagements

### 01 — Brand Sprint — from €2,400

**You need clarity**

*For when things feel unclear.*

There's potential in the brand, but the positioning, the visual direction and the
messaging don't agree with each other. We find what's pulling them apart and spend
the next 30 days on the changes that count.

**We'll help you:**

- Find the gaps in your current brand
- Clarify positioning and messaging
- Establish a focused visual direction
- Fix the highest-impact brand problems
- Leave you with a prioritised action plan

**Engagement:** `2 weeks` · `Focused engagement` · `Fixed scope`

**CTA:** Let's find the problem

**Upgrade path:** The action plan is written to be executed as a Brand System if
that turns out to be the right next step.

---

### 02 — Brand System — from €7,800 *(most chosen)*

**You need a system that scales**

*When your brand needs to work without you.*

If every new asset needs another decision, more templates won't help. What your
team needs is a system. We build the foundation they can use, repeat and grow with.

**We'll help you:**

- Build a coherent identity system
- Define messaging and communication rules
- Create reusable production components
- Make your brand easier for your team to execute
- Document the system for future work

**Engagement:** `6–8 weeks` · `Collaborative project` · `Fixed scope`

**CTA:** Build our system

**Why it's the default:** Most clients arrive in the Sprint and land here. The
card is highlighted because it solves the most common version of the problem,
not because it is the most expensive.

---

### 03 — Rhythm Retainer — €2,400 / month

**You need ongoing momentum**

*For teams that need a partner, not another vendor.*

You've got the brand system. The hard part is keeping it moving. We sit alongside
your team to decide what matters first, produce what's needed, and keep the
system current as the business changes.

**We'll help you:**

- Prioritise upcoming brand and creative needs
- Produce work against an agreed cadence
- Review what's working and what isn't
- Improve the system as new needs appear
- Provide priority access when something matters

**Engagement:** `3-month minimum` · `Ongoing partnership` · `Monthly`

**CTA:** Talk about what you need

## How to Route an Enquiry

- **Cold lead, no brand yet** → Diagnose on the call. Sprint is the usual entry
  point; the action plan names the upgrade.
- **Brand exists, output is messy** → Brand System. Retainer optional at handoff.
- **System live, needs throughput** → Rhythm Retainer.
- **None of the above** → Say so. A written recommendation is still worth
  sending, and it is how the relationship starts.

Every engagement starts the same way: a short call, a written scope, a fixed
price. Retainers only begin once there's a reason for one.
